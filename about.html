About AI Legal Document Analyzer

The AI-Based Legal Document Analysis and Question Answering System is an intelligent document processing platform that uses Artificial Intelligence, Large Language Models, Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG), and semantic search to understand legal documents.

🧠 Artificial Intelligence

Uses modern AI models to understand legal language and provide context-aware answers.

📚 RAG Technology

Retrieval Augmented Generation retrieves relevant document sections before generating answers.

⚖️ Legal Analysis

Automatically identifies clauses, obligations, deadlines, and potential risks.

📊 Smart Reporting

Creates professional PDF reports containing document insights.

Technology Stack

  • Python
  • Flask
  • Ollama Llama 3.1
  • Retrieval Augmented Generation (RAG)
  • FAISS Vector Database

  • PyMuPDF PDF Processing
